Why inqa exists

The awareness was visible.
The conversation wasn't.

Every company we worked with had the same gap. They'd invest in awareness — events, ads, content, social — and they'd create real curiosity. People wanted to know more.

But the moment curiosity hit, it left. It went to ChatGPT. To Reddit threads. To YouTube. To peer forums. The questions were happening — just not anywhere the brand could see, shape, or respond to.

  • ChatGPT
  • Reddit
  • YouTube
  • Peer forums
  • Competitor sites

The brand paid for the awareness. Someone else captured the conversation. And by the time the person showed up at a form or a sales call, the narrative had already been set — by someone else.

inqa exists because those conversations belong with you.
