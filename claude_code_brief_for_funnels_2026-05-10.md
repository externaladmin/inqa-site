## What this page is

A use-case landing page targeting growth leaders running high-volume paid acquisition into a high-consideration sale. The audience reads it after clicking from an ad, an outbound email, or a referral.

The page makes one big architectural claim: **the self-serve intake form is the problem. The conversation is the answer the funnel was substituting for.**

---

## Audience for this page

Narrower than the homepage. Likely:
- Head of Growth, VP Demand Gen, Performance Marketing leader
- At a company with high paid acquisition spend
- Where conversion event is a booked demo or paid signup
- Series B and up, typically

They are skeptical of rip-and-replace pitches. They want concrete numbers, not abstract benefits.
